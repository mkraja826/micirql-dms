CapDent Account Deletion

Delete your CapDent Android app account and understand exactly what happens to clinic data.

Delete directly inside the CapDent app

  1. Open the CapDent Android app.
  2. Open More → Legal & Account → Delete Account & Data.
  3. Read the deletion warning shown for your clinic role.
  4. Enter the confirmation text displayed by CapDent and confirm deletion.

The in-app deletion action permanently deletes the applicable CapDent account without requiring a separate support request.

What is deleted for Owner or Head Doctor

In CapDent, Owner and Head Doctor are clinic-authority roles. If an Owner or Head Doctor chooses Delete Clinic & Account, CapDent warns that the action will permanently delete the clinic workspace together with the clinic-authority account.

This includes clinic-owned data associated with that workspace, such as patients, appointments, visits, treatments, dental charts, prescriptions, X-rays/photos and other clinical files, invoices, payment records, reports, merchant-account configuration, clinic settings and staff access, subject to any records that must legally be retained.

This action cannot be undone. CapDent tells the Owner or Head Doctor to export or retain records the clinic is required to keep before confirming deletion.

What is deleted for Doctor, Receptionist or other staff

Deleting a Doctor, Receptionist or other non-authority staff account deletes that user's CapDent login/profile and clinic access only.

The clinic's patients, appointments, treatments, dental records, files, invoices, payments, reports and other clinic-owned data remain with the clinic.

If you no longer have the app

You can also request deletion of your CapDent account from the email address associated with the account. CapDent Support may request only the minimum information necessary to verify that you are authorized to make the request.

Records that may need to be retained

Dental clinics or service operators may be required to retain certain medical, billing, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution or legal records. Where retention is legally required, access may be restricted or information may be de-identified where appropriate, and only the information necessary for the applicable obligation is retained for the required period.

Security

Never send your password, OTP, UPI PIN, payment credentials, authentication tokens or unnecessary patient information with a deletion request. CapDent will request only the minimum information required for verification.
