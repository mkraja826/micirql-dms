Account controls

Delete Account & Data

CapDent provides an in-app and web-accessible process for requesting account and related data deletion.

How to request deletion

  1. In the CapDent Android app, open More → Legal & Account → Delete Account & Data; or use the email option below.
  2. Send the deletion request from the email address associated with your CapDent account where possible.
  3. CapDent Support may ask for the minimum information necessary to verify that the requester is authorized to make the request.
  4. After verification, CapDent will review the account and associated data and confirm the applicable deletion or retention outcome.

Request deletion by email

Data covered by a request

A verified request may cover the user's login account and related clinic information, including clinic profile data, staff access, patient records, appointments, visits, uploaded prescriptions, X-rays, photos, payment records and reminders where deletion is legally and operationally permitted.

Records that may need to be retained

Dental clinics and service operators may be required to retain certain medical, billing, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution or legal records. When retention is required, CapDent may restrict access, de-identify information where appropriate, or retain only the records necessary for the applicable obligation instead of deleting them immediately.

Clinic ownership and staff accounts

Deleting an individual staff login does not automatically mean that patient or clinic records legitimately belonging to the clinic should be deleted. Requests affecting an entire clinic workspace may require verification from an authorized clinic owner or equivalent administrator.

Security of deletion requests

Do not send passwords, payment credentials, raw authentication tokens or unnecessary patient information by email. CapDent Support should request only the minimum information required to verify the requester securely.
