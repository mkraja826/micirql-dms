Ask your clinic a question.

CapDent AI is designed for authorized owner-level dashboard use, turning everyday clinic records into useful business and operational answers without replacing clinical judgement.

“How much did we collect this month?”
“Which treatments generated the most revenue?”
“Show patients with pending dues.”
“How are we performing compared with last month?”
Owner:
How is the clinic performing this month?

Owner:
Which patients still need payment follow-up?
CapDent AII can surface patients with outstanding balances from the clinic’s authorized records so the owner can review follow-up priorities.

AI examples are illustrative. Clinical decisions remain with qualified dental professionals.

Frequently asked

Before your clinic starts.

Straight answers about CapDent V25 and the platform.

Can a clinic start CapDent for free?

Yes. The free tier includes core Android clinic management for up to 100 patients and 1 GB of free cloud storage.

What happens after 100 patients?

Clinics can move to the ₹799 Cloud plan for unlimited patient capacity, dashboard access and expanded cloud clinic management.

Who can use CapDent AI chat?

AI chat is included in the ₹1,499 Intelligence plan and is designed for authorized clinic owner or head-doctor use inside the dashboard.

What is included in the ₹2,499 plan?

The AI Imaging plan builds on the Intelligence plan with high-end AI imaging and advanced dental image intelligence capabilities.

Does CapDent include a dental chart and clinical files?

Yes. CapDent is designed around connected patient records including treatment workflow, dental charting, prescriptions, X-rays and clinical images.

Is the information shown in the product preview real?

No. Names, counts and financial values in the website preview are fictional demonstration data.
