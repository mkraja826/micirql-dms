Frequently asked

Before your clinic starts.

Straight answers about CapDent V28 and the platform.

Can a clinic start CapDent for free?

Yes. The free tier includes core Android clinic management for up to 100 patients, 1 GB of cloud storage and up to 150 clinical uploads.

Where does the dental chart live?

The dental chart is designed to be accessed from the patient profile so doctors can update it when clinically required without slowing the Add Visit workflow.

Does CapDent support a dental assistant role?

Yes. CapDent includes a Dental Assistant role with the same workflow and permissions as Receptionist.

Where will CapDent AI be available?

AI is planned for the owner/head-doctor web dashboard only. It is not part of the Android clinical workflow.

What happens after 100 patients?

Clinics can move to the ₹799 Cloud plan for unlimited patient capacity, dashboard access and expanded cloud clinic management.

Is the information shown in the product preview real?

No. Names, counts and financial values in the website preview are fictional demonstration data.
